body.glb - 3D human muscle body model
=====================================

body.glb is an adapted work (meshes selected, merged into 16 named groups, cut, simplified,
re-normalised and quantised) of the following openly licensed datasets:

1) Z-Anatomy - Muscular system and Skeletal system models
   Files used: MuscularSystem100.fbx, SkeletalSystem100.fbx
   Source:  https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy  (branch PC-Version, Resources/Models/FBX)
            project: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy
   Authors: Z-Anatomy project (Gauthier Kervyn, Lluis Vinent Juanico and contributors)
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

2) BodyParts3D (Z-Anatomy's models are derived from it)
   Source:  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
   License page: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   (BodyParts3D was formerly distributed under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP; the current license
   page, checked 2026-11-11, states CC Attribution 4.0 International.)

Changes made: only skeletal muscles, bones and a few tendons/aponeuroses were kept;
internal organs, vessels, nerves, fasciae, bursae and deep muscles were removed. Muscles were
merged into the groups chest, shoulders, biceps, triceps, forearms, abs, obliques, traps,
lats, lowerback, glutes, quads, adductors, hams, calves, plus "body". The oblique
aponeuroses over the rectus abdominis, the lumbar part of the latissimus dorsi aponeurosis
over the erector spinae, and the iliotibial tract were cut away so the underlying groups are
visible. The meshes were simplified (meshoptimizer), smooth normals were recomputed, and the
model was rescaled to 2 units tall.
