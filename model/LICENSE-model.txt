body.glb / body-female.glb - 3D human body models (skin-covered athletic male and female figures with muscle groups)
==========================================================================================================

body.glb (male) and body-female.glb (female) are adapted works (a smooth skin surface generated from the muscle and bone meshes,
split into 16 named groups, simplified, re-normalised and quantised) of the following openly
licensed datasets:

1) Z-Anatomy - Muscular system and Skeletal system models
   Files used: MuscularSystem100.fbx, SkeletalSystem100.fbx
   Source:  https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy  (branch PC-Version, Resources/Models/FBX)
            project: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy
   Authors: Z-Anatomy project (Gauthier Kervyn, Lluis Vinent Juanico and contributors)
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

2) BodyParts3D (Z-Anatomy's models are derived from it)
   Source:  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
   License page: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   (BodyParts3D was formerly distributed under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP; the current license
   page, checked 2026-11-11, states CC Attribution 4.0 International.)

Changes made: skeletal muscles and bones (no organs, vessels, nerves or fasciae) were re-posed
(arms abducted about the shoulder, forearms pronated, wrists straightened, legs slightly apart),
voxelised and wrapped in a continuous skin surface (morphological closing with a soft-tissue offset,
Gaussian smoothing, iso-surface extraction, Taubin smoothing). Shallow channels were carved along the
boundaries between neighbouring source muscles so the muscle separations stay readable. The
individual muscles are no longer separate meshes. The skull and face were replaced by a smooth, bald,
faceless sculpted head (generated, not taken from the datasets).
body-female.glb was made from the same source with smooth spatial warps (narrower shoulders and
ribcage, waist taper, wider pelvis, slimmer arms and neck), generated breast, hip and glute volumes,
a softer head and softer muscle channels. No genital or nipple detail is modelled on either figure.
Each surface was simplified (meshoptimizer) to about 120k triangles and split into the regions chest,
shoulders, biceps, triceps, forearms, abs, obliques, traps, lats, lowerback, glutes, quads, adductors,
hams, calves, plus "body", by projecting each skin area onto the nearest Z-Anatomy muscle (with the
oblique aponeuroses over the rectus abdominis and the lumbar latissimus aponeurosis cut away for that
projection). The models were rescaled to 2 units tall.
