body.glb / body-female.glb - 3D human body models (smooth athletic mannequins with muscle groups)
==================================================================================================

Geometry
--------
Both models are built from the MakeHuman base mesh and modelling targets:

  MakeHuman (base mesh base.obj, macrodetails / breast / torso / armslegs / stomach targets,
  default rig weights default_weights.mhw)
  Source:  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (makehuman/data)
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (the MakeHuman assets - base mesh, proxies, targets and modifiers -
           were released as CC0; see license.txt / LICENSE.ASSETS.md in that repository).
  Original copyright (C) Manuel Bastioni and the MakeHuman team; released as CC0.

Changes: young adult male / female targets with raised muscle and slightly low weight, local muscle
targets, arms re-posed about the shoulder with the MakeHuman rig weights, legs brought slightly
together, one level of Catmull-Clark subdivision, nipples smoothed. The head was replaced by a smooth
faceless mannequin head generated from the MakeHuman head silhouette (voxel remesh + smoothing; no
facial features). Plain shorts (and a sports top on the female) were generated as cloth shells on the
skin. Simplified (meshoptimizer), split into the regions chest, shoulders, biceps, triceps, forearms,
abs, obliques, traps, lats, lowerback, glutes, quads, adductors, hams, calves plus "body"
(cloth nodes are marked with extras.fabric), rescaled to 2 units tall and quantised.

Muscle-group regions
--------------------
Arm and leg regions come from the MakeHuman rig. The torso regions (chest, abs, obliques, traps,
lats, lowerback, glutes) were transferred by nearest-surface lookup from an earlier figure derived
from:

  Z-Anatomy - Muscular system and Skeletal system models
  Source:  https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy (Resources/Models/FBX), project https://github.com/Z-Anatomy
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  Z-Anatomy is based on BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0
  (https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html).

No Z-Anatomy geometry is contained in the files; only the region labelling of the torso was derived
from it. To stay on the safe side the model files are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and credit both
sources. The ShareAlike terms apply to the model files and modified versions of them, not to the
viewer code (body3d.js) or the rest of the app.
